Export Products

Products, tasveerein, aur har product ka apna Google-friendly page — sab ek ZIP me.

Pehle admin me login karein. Warna Firestore products parhne nahi dega. Yahan se login karein, phir wapas aayein.

Qadam 1 — Products parhein

Ye sirf parhta hai. Firestore me kuch nahi badalta.

0products
0tasveerein
0nikalni hain

Qadam 2 — ZIP download karein

ZIP me char cheezein hongi:

  1. products-data.js — chhoti file
  2. assets/products/ — saari tasveerein alag files
  3. Har product ka apna HTML page — Google ke liye
  4. sitemap.xml — naye pages ke saath

Qadam 3 — GitHub par upload

  1. ZIP kholein
  2. Andar ki saari cheezein repo par upload karein
  3. 2–3 minute rukein
  4. Website incognito me kholein

Naye products ke liye: Google Search Console kholein → URL Inspection → naye page ka pata daal kar Request Indexing dabayein. Warna Google ko dhoondne me hafte lag jate hain.
